## Current technical behaviour (baseline for approval, not a time-based policy)

The current implementation behaves as follows. These limits describe software capacity and handling; they do **not** establish how long client information may legally or contractually be retained.

| Data class | Current implemented behaviour | What still requires approval |
|---|---|---|
| Active issue memory | Holds up to **2,000** records. When the active set exceeds this limit, older records are moved into the archive rather than silently deleted. | The approved time period for keeping active records and the circumstances for removing them. |
| Archived issue memory | Bounded append-only archive of up to **10,000** records. When the archive is full, new records are rejected rather than silently deleting older evidence. | The approved archive retention period, export cadence, deletion method and treatment of backups. |
| Audit history | Append-only JSONL hash-chain history with signed checkpoint export, verification and restore support. | The time period for retaining audit history and checkpoints, independent storage and deletion controls. |
| Backups and exports | Tenant-scoped issue archive exports and signed audit checkpoint exports are supported by the software. Independent off-host storage and restore still require operational verification. | Backup frequency, location, administrators, retention, replica handling and verified deletion. |
| Operational logs / evidence bundles | Generated by the running services and acceptance procedures. No client-approved time-based schedule is established here. | Retention period, access, redaction, legal hold and deletion. |

**Important:** Do not convert the 2,000 / 10,000 record capacity limits into days or months. Do not enable automatic purge or claim a retention policy is approved until the authorized client approver signs the schedule below. Until then, preserve the existing fail-safe bounded-store behaviour.
